# Playtest checklist (v0.3)

Three short sessions, a phone each. Note the time in minutes; do not coach the player.

**Session 1: first ten minutes (new farm, no hints from us)**
- Did they find what to do first without asking? What did they tap that did nothing?
- Minute they first repaired something, first got a hen, first filled an order.
- Did the red "!" and the coins over ripe things make sense? Did they use the "Next:" chip?
- Anything that felt like chores: count the taps to harvest and sow six beds.

**Session 2: the evening (come back after a few hours)**
- Was there something waiting (coins, rent, goals)? Did it feel like a gift or a pile of duty?
- Did they use the free Hurry? Did June and Pip's help read as help, not as cheating?

**Session 3: day two or three**
- Where did the pace feel too fast or too slow (goals, levels, the school at day 2–5)?
- Did a full barn ever block them? Did money ever run out so they could not repair or sow?
- Ellis's letters: did they read them, and did they want to know more?

**Questions to ask at the end**
1. What was the most fun minute? The most boring?
2. Did you ever feel rushed or guilty for leaving?
3. What would you want next: more animals, more people, decorating, fishing, something else?

Write the answers in docs/PLAYTEST-LOG.md with the date; tune numbers in src/content/economy.mjs and run `npm run sim`.
