# Evaluation of Farm Village v0.3 against other farm and village games (2026-10-07)

Compared from what is publicly known of Hay Day, Stardew Valley, Cozy Grove and Animal Crossing: New Horizons. I did not
play them side by side here; this is a design comparison, to be checked by a playtest.

## What already works
- **Short loops, clear rewards** (Hay Day): crops, trees, hens and goods now take 20–55 s, with a few 3–5 minute premium things (pumpkin, peach, cow, carrot cake). A coin over every ripe thing says "tap me".
- **A place with a past** (Cozy Grove, Stardew's community centre): the restored village, the broken market, Ellis's letters and the school give a reason to repair and a visible before and after.
- **Gentle pressure** (Animal Crossing): wear never stops anything, nothing is lost while you are away, a full barn sells the overflow instead of blocking you.
- **Guidance**: the Next chip, the status stack, red "!" on broken things, Ada's first session.
- **Many small systems that feed each other**: fish, trees, hens, bread, the truck, rent and tips, neighbours.

## Where it falls short
1. **Story is passive.** Chapters and heart scenes arrive by level; there is no running mystery to pull you on. Stardew and Cozy Grove always have one open question.
2. **No quest log.** Hay Day shows three to five goals at all times; here the goal is one line.
3. **No reason to come back tomorrow** beyond the streak flower and the Today gift: no daily "hurry", no weekly event.
4. **The player is thin.** You are a figure that walks, but there is no outfit, name or home to make yours.
5. **Little to collect.** Fish, trees and decorations have no album, so there is no completion drive.
6. **People are mostly speakers.** Taps give a line or an order; few ask for a small favour or react to what you built.
7. **Old saves miss the new village** (market, truck, pond).
8. **Balance is bot-tested only**: school day 2 (keen) to day 5 (casual) is a guess until real players try it.

## Suggested improvements (ranked by value for effort)
1. **Quest log** with three live goals (a repair, a delivery, a collection), each paying coins and hearts; the Next chip already finds the first step.
2. **Ellis's trail:** one letter per chapter that opens one more clue (where he went upriver), ending with a festival at the school.
3. **Daily "hurry" token** and a small daily chest, plus a weekly village goal everybody helps with.
4. **Fish and tree albums** (collections with a reward for each page), reusing the family album.
5. **Favours:** a villager at the pond or the market asks for one specific thing ("a perch for my supper") for a bigger reward and hearts.
6. **Player customisation:** a name, an outfit colour and a choice of figure, from Willowmere's hero bodies.
7. **Migration** that gives old saves the market, the truck and the pond once.
8. **Playtest the pace** with three real sessions, then tune.

## Done in this round
The fence rule is gone (hens just need their coop repaired), a full barn sells the surplus and lets you sell anything from the barn, rent is worth having and families leave tips, the order board is a compact list with a short Sell button, and the top bars are rearranged (turn and settings top right, status stack top left, the album lives in Settings).

## Update (v0.3d)
Built from the list above: goals and favours, the weekly goal and daily hurry, Ellis's trail and festival, fish and fruit albums, player customisation (name, figure, shirt) and a migration for old saves. Still open: playtesting the pace with real sessions.
