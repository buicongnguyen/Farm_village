# AR-009 lucky-discovery keepsakes

Three original keepsakes for the one-time discoveries (brief: `docs/CLAUDE-DISCOVERY-HANDOFF.md` on the logic branch).
They live in their own kit, `public/assets/models/discovery-props.glb`, which nothing loads until a discovery card or
reveal asks for it.

| Discovery | Stand-in icon now | New prop and icon | Triangles | Size (w × d × h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pond-tin`: a little tin from the pond | `ui:coin` | `lucky_tin` | 1,076 | 0.18 × 0.21 × 0.14 m |
| `pond-keepsake`: the fish on the button | `perch` | `lucky_button` | 1,076 | 0.16 × 0.15 × 0.16 m |
| `stone-keepsake`: a keepsake beneath a stone | `tool:clear` | `lucky_box` | 1,104 | 0.22 × 0.17 × 0.15 m |
| `street-thanks`: a thank-you for Village Street | `ui:coin` | unchanged: existing coin art | - | - |

- `icons-before-after.png`: each stand-in beside the new icon at 160 px and 48 px (actual phone size), on a dark (dusk
  HUD) and a light (paper card) panel.
- `keepsakes-blender.jpg`: a close-up of the three props together, rendered in Blender under the kit's preview lights
  on the game's lawn colour (`#72bd3e`). The studio lights make the grass read lighter than in the game.

Look: warm painted colours (teal, cream, red, soft blue cloth, honey wood); brass and gold only on rims, one clasp and
a token coin or two; one small pale-gold four-point glint each. Three different silhouettes: a round tin with its lid
up, a fish button against a pouch, a square box with its lid open. The button's tiny engraved pond is on its back,
facing the pouch in this pose. A first review pass judged the earlier versions too close to a treasure chest and a
money bag; the red chest lining, brass corners, coin piles and cream sack were removed.
